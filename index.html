Samson Lam

I am a Master of Science in Quantitative Finance student at the Gabelli School of Business, Fordham University. I graduated from the Quantitative Finance and Risk Management Science program at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) with First-Class Honours and minors in Mathematics and Statistics.

My research interests focus on financial econometrics and the statistical theory of time series, particularly the unique challenges posed by financial data. Asset returns frequently exhibit heavy tails, skewness, jumps and complex volatility patterns such as clustering, leverage effects and long memory.

I am especially interested in developing efficient estimation techniques for models with intractable likelihoods and in devising statistical methods to distinguish between observationally similar mechanisms, such as regime switching and genuine long memory in volatility. Beyond estimation and identification, my broader interests extend to forecasting, risk and continuous-time modeling of financial processes.

Outside research, I enjoy cooking, baking, playing badminton and traveling.

Code and reports for my projects are on GitHub.
