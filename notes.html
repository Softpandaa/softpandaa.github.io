Study notes in mathematics, statistics and quantitative finance from my undergraduate and graduate materials are organized by topic:

  • Real and Functional Analysis leads to Complex and Fourier Analysis
  • Complex and Fourier Analysis leads to Differential Equations
  • Differential Equations leads to Numerical Analysis and Statistical Inference, Regression and Time Series
  • Optimization, Operations Research and Games leads to Statistical Inference, Regression and Time Series
  • Statistical Inference, Regression and Time Series leads to Machine Learning and Stochastic Processes and Calculus
  • Stochastic Processes and Calculus leads to Investments and Quantitative Asset Management and Derivatives
  • Numerical Analysis leads to Derivatives
  • Financial Markets and Corporate Finance leads to Investments and Quantitative Asset Management and Fixed Income, Interest Rates and Credit
  • Derivatives leads to Fixed Income, Interest Rates and Credit
